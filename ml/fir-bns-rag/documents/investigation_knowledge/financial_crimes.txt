[STAGE: Forensic Accounting & Audit]
ACTION: Requisition audited financial statements, ledger entries, bank account transactions, and tax filings under Section 94 BNSS.
PURPOSE: Identify systematic diversion of funds, shell company transfers, or forged invoices.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Forensic audit report, certified bank ledger extracts, shell company corporate registry filings.

[STAGE: Document Authentication]
ACTION: Obtain questioned signatures, cheques, or financial contracts and send to State Examiner of Questioned Documents (SEQD).
PURPOSE: Verify authenticity of handwriting and detect forgery.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: SEQD handwriting expert opinion report, specimen signature sheets.
