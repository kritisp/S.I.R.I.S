[STAGE: Crime Scene Examination]
ACTION: Inspect entry and exit points of the premises (doors, windows, roof, locks).
PURPOSE: Identify method of entry, forced entry marks, or tool marks used by housebreakers.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Scene photographs, tool mark impressions, lock defect notes.

[STAGE: Evidence Recovery & Forensics]
ACTION: Collect latent fingerprints from touchpoints (doorknobs, lock handles, safe handles, glass surfaces).
PURPOSE: Lift latent friction ridge impressions of perpetrators for AFIS database comparison.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Fingerprint cards, dusting powder lifts, forensic technician report.

[STAGE: Property Recovery & Search]
ACTION: Obtain detailed description, serial numbers, invoice receipts, and photograph records of stolen jewellery/assets.
PURPOSE: Issue alert notices to local goldsmiths, pawn shops, and secondhand market monitoring channels under BNSS Section 107.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Stolen property register entry, goldsmith alert notice copies, purchase receipts.

[STAGE: CCTV & Digital Intelligence]
ACTION: Retrieve and secure CCTV footage from nearby cameras covering approach and escape routes during the crime window.
PURPOSE: Establish physical identification and timeline of suspects.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Digital DVR footage export, Section 63 BSHA electronic evidence certificate.

[STAGE: Informant & Neighborhood Canvassing]
ACTION: Conduct door-to-door neighborhood inquiry and examine local informants regarding suspicious persons or vehicles observed near scene.
PURPOSE: Identify potential eyewitnesses and suspect escape vehicles.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Witness statements recorded under BNSS Section 180.
