[STAGE: Chain of Custody & Malkhana Storage]
ACTION: Seal seized articles with official police impression seal and log item into Malkhana Register (Form No. 108).
PURPOSE: Maintain tamper-proof chain of custody from seizure to court trial presentation.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Malkhana register entry receipt, sealed parcel tag memo.

[STAGE: Forensic Dispatch]
ACTION: Dispatch sealed biological/digital samples to Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL) via special messenger.
PURPOSE: Prevent degradation or tampering during transit.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: FSL acknowledgement receipt, messenger transit log.
