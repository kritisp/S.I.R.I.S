[STAGE: Substance Securing & Seizure]
ACTION: Secure the seized suspected contraband substance and prevent any contamination, leakage, or loss.
PURPOSE: Establish corpus delicti of the drug offense and ensure physical quantity is verified.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Seizure memo (Panchnama) signed by independent witnesses, physical package seal, weighing records.

[STAGE: Sample Collection & Forensic Dispatch]
ACTION: Draw representative duplicate samples of the seized contraband in the presence of a magistrate and dispatch to Forensic Science Laboratory (FSL).
PURPOSE: Confirm the chemical composition, purity, and exact classification of the narcotics substance under NDPS guidelines.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: FSL sample dispatch form, magistrate inventory certificate under Section 105 BNSS, FSL receipt acknowledgement.

[STAGE: Source & Supply Chain Investigation]
ACTION: Interrogate the accused to identify the drug source network, suppliers, carriers, and prospective buyers.
PURPOSE: Map the distribution channels, identify contraband storehouses, and intercept active smuggling modules.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Accused disclosure statements, call details records (CDR) map, suspect link analysis charts.
