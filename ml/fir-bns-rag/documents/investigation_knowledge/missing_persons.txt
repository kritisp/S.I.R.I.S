[STAGE: Immediate Alert & Lookup]
ACTION: Upload missing person photograph, physical description, clothing notes, and biological details to TrackChild portal and ZIPNET database.
PURPOSE: Broadcast nationwide alert across railway stations, bus terminals, and shelter homes.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: TrackChild registration portal receipt, ZIPNET broadcast alert log.

[STAGE: CDR & Location Tracking]
ACTION: Obtain Call Detail Records (CDR) and Tower Location dump of missing person's mobile number for last 72 hours.
PURPOSE: Track last known physical location and final contacts before disappearance.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: CDR analysis report, cell tower location map.
