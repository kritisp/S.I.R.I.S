[STAGE: Spot Inspection & Photography]
ACTION: Conduct systematic grid search of crime scene and capture 360-degree high-definition photographs/videos.
PURPOSE: Document spatial relationship of physical evidence before disturbance.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Crime scene photo album, spot map drawing, audio-video recording media under BNSS 105.

[STAGE: Witness & Informant Interview]
ACTION: Interview first responder, complainant, and neighbors at scene.
PURPOSE: Record immediate spontaneous disclosures before memory fade or intimidation.
EVIDENCE_GENERATED: Initial statement notes, first responder duty log.
